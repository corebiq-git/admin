FAQ

Common questions, clearly answered.

Quick answers about services, enquiries and this website.

What services does CM Filings provide?

CM Filings presents services across taxation and GST, accounting and finance, business registration, compliance, business advisory and technology-enabled business solutions.

How do I start an enquiry?

Use the Contact page and prepare an email enquiry. Include your name, mobile number, email, relevant service and a short description of your requirement.

Can I enquire about business registration?

Yes. The Business Registration service page covers structure, setup, documentation, application workflow and post-registration planning at a high level.

Do you provide accounting and compliance support together?

The service architecture is designed so accounting, finance and recurring compliance can be considered together where appropriate.

Is the website form storing my information?

The current static prototype uses a mailto workflow. Submitting the form opens your default email client; the website itself does not store the form submission.

Where is CM Filings located?

The public contact address is Shakthi Complex, Mahe, Kannur District, Kerala 670672.

Can the website be connected to a CRM or backend later?

Yes. The current kit is intentionally static and can later be connected to a secure backend, CRM, appointment system or enquiry API without changing the information architecture.

Does this website provide legal or tax advice by itself?

No. Website content is general information. Specific tax, legal, accounting or compliance decisions should be evaluated based on your facts and applicable rules.
