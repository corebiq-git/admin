Business Flow

One business journey. Connected from beginning to end.

Business Flow is the operating concept behind COREBIQ: connect the customer journey, team work and financial events into one understandable process.

02QualifyCustomer, requirement and opportunity
03CommitQuote, order, booking or project
04ExecuteTasks, people, inventory and delivery
